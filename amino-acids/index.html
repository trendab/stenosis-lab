Amino Acid Side Chains

Grundlagen der Biologie 1 · Locher's groups. Start with line formulas as on his slides, then switch to the stick model used in the exam peptide.

Drawing style

Every corner and line end is a carbon. H on carbon is not drawn. Hashed wedge at Cα = side chain points behind the page (L-configuration in this orientation).

C grey N blue O red S yellow
How to read a stick model
  1. Find the backbone: N – Cα – C(=O), repeating. Read the chain from N to C. Everything hanging off Cα is the side chain.
  2. S (yellow) at the end of the chain: Cys. S in the middle with a carbon after it: Met.
  3. Grey only: count carbons and find the branch. 1 C = Ala; 3 C branched at Cβ = Val; 4 C branched at Cγ = Leu; 4 C branched at Cβ = Ile.
  4. One O: Ser (straight) or Thr (branched, extra C).
  5. Two atoms at the tip: O + O = acid, O + N = amide. Short (1 C before the tip carbon) = Asp/Asn, longer = Glu/Gln.
  6. Long chain, one N at the end: Lys. Three N at the end: Arg.
  7. Rings: carbon hexagon = Phe; plus an OH = Tyr; 5-ring with two N = His; two fused rings, one N = Trp.
  8. A ring that closes back onto the backbone N: Pro. No side chain at all: Gly.
